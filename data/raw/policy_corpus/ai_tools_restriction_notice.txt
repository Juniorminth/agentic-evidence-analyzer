AI Tools Restriction Notice:
For the final project, students may not submit code that was generated by an AI tool unless the instructor gives written approval.

Allowed AI Use:
Students may still use AI tools for brainstorming, conceptual explanation, and debugging questions. The restriction applies specifically to submitting AI-generated code as part of the final project.

Documentation Requirement:
If AI tools are used during development, students must describe how they were used in the final report.
