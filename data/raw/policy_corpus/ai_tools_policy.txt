Use of AI Tools:
Students may use AI tools for brainstorming, debugging, explanation, and learning support. Students must understand and be able to explain all submitted work.

Generated Code Policy:
Generated code may be used only after the student reviews, tests, and understands it. Students are responsible for the correctness of any generated code they submit.

Citation Policy:
When AI tools substantially influence submitted code or explanations, students should cite the use of those tools.
